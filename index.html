Bread worth waiting for.

We bake sourdough the slow way with only natural ingredients, long fermentation, and lots of care.

Why Sourdough?

Sourdough is fermented naturally using a wild starter and given 18 to 36 hours of slow fermentation.

Made With Only Real Ingredients

Kirkland Organic Flour, King Arthur Whole Wheat Flour, water, salt, and Terra Delyssa Extra Virgin Olive Oil for our focaccia.

Our Bread

Sourdough loaf and sourdough focaccia are available. Little baguettes and flavored loaves are coming soon.

Why We Started

We were looking for an alternative to store-bought bread. The ingredient lists were long and confusing, so we started baking our own with simple ingredients and patience.
