Why Sourdough?

Sourdough is fermented naturally using a wild starter.
It takes time, but that’s what makes it special.

The long fermentation gives the dough time to develop slowly, which some people find easier to digest, and creates a depth of flavor that commercial yeast just can’t replicate.

Why We Started

We were simply looking for an alternative to store-bought bread.
The ingredient lists were long, confusing, and full of things we couldn’t pronounce.
We couldn’t find bread that felt right for our family.

So we started baking our own — with only real ingredients and lots of patience.
What began as a personal mission quickly turned into a passion
we knew we had to share.

This is more than bread. It’s how we choose to feed the people we love —
and how we hope to serve you.

FAQ

How long does sourdough bread stay fresh?

It is best in the first few days. Slice and freeze it for longer storage.

How should I store my bread?

Keep it at room temperature, cut-side down or in a bread bag. Avoid the refrigerator.

When do you bake?

We bake in small weekly batches for Saturday pickup.

Where is pickup?

Local pickup details are shared with confirmed orders.

Do you take custom orders?

Ask us about special requests and we’ll let you know what we can do.

Do you use commercial yeast?

No. Our sourdough is naturally leavened with our starter.
