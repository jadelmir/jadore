Why Sourdough?

Why Sourdough?

Sourdough is naturally fermented using a living starter. It takes time, but that’s what makes it special.

We let the dough ferment slowly so flavor and structure can develop naturally — creating bread with character that rushed dough simply can’t replicate.

Why We Started

We were simply looking for an alternative to store-bought bread. The ingredient lists were long, confusing, and full of things that didn’t feel right.

So we started baking our own — with simple ingredients, a living starter, and lots of patience. What began in our kitchen became something we wanted to share with our community.

This is more than bread. It’s a slower, simpler way of making food for the people we love.
