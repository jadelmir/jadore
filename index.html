Why Sourdough?

Time is our secret ingredient.

Sourdough is naturally fermented using a living starter. We give the dough time to develop rather than rushing it, creating deeper flavor, better texture, and the character that makes real sourdough special.

Our Story

Why We Started

We were simply looking for an alternative to store-bought bread.

The ingredient lists were long, confusing, and full of things that didn’t feel right. We couldn’t find bread that felt right for our family.

So we started baking our own — with simple ingredients, a living starter, and lots of patience. What began in our kitchen became something we wanted to share with our community.

This is more than bread to us. It’s a slower, simpler way of making food for the people we love.

FAQ

What makes sourdough different?

Sourdough uses a naturally fermented starter instead of commercial yeast. The long fermentation develops the flavor and structure that make it unique.

How long does sourdough stay fresh?

It is best in the first few days. Store it cut-side down or in a bread bag at room temperature, or slice and freeze it for longer storage.

How should I store my bread?

Avoid the refrigerator. Keep it at room temperature for short-term storage or freeze slices for later.

Do you use commercial yeast?

No. Our sourdough is naturally leavened with our starter and given plenty of time to ferment.

Do you offer focaccia every week?

Our current menu includes sourdough loaves and sourdough focaccia. Availability can vary by weekly batch.
