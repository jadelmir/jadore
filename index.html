Why Sourdough? Time is our secret ingredient.

Our sourdough begins with flour, water, salt, and a wild starter. We give it 18 to 36 hours of slow fermentation, then shape each loaf by hand and bake it fresh.

Just a few honest ingredients.

Our sourdough loaves use salt, water, Kirkland Organic Flour, and King Arthur Whole Wheat in the starter. Our sourdough focaccia uses the same ingredients plus Terra Delyssa Extra Virgin Olive Oil.

Our Weekly Bakes

Sourdough loaves and sourdough focaccia are available now. Little baguettes and flavored loaves are coming soon.

Why We Started

We were looking for an alternative to store-bought bread. Too many ingredients did not feel right, so we started baking our own with simple ingredients and a slower process. What began in our kitchen became something we are proud to share with our community.

Made with love by Jad and Pia.

FAQ

Our sourdough is naturally leavened with a wild starter and fermented slowly. Store bread at room temperature for short-term use or slice and freeze it for longer storage.
