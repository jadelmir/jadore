Slow fermentation breaks down gluten and phytic acid—making it easier on the body.

Our Story

Why We Started

We were trying to find an alternative to store-bought bread.

Too many ingredients didn’t feel right.

So we began making sourdough with simple, honest ingredients and a slower process. What began in our kitchen grew into something we’re proud to share with our community.

Thank you for supporting real, local, and small.

— jad and pia ♡

FAQ

What makes sourdough different?

It is naturally fermented using a wild starter and given time to develop.

Where do you bake?

We bake locally in small batches.

How should I store my bread?

Keep it at room temperature for the first few days or slice and freeze it.
