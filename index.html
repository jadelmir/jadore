Our Story

Why We Started

We were trying to find an alternative to store-bought bread.

Too many ingredients didn’t feel right. The labels were long, and we wanted something simpler — something we actually felt good bringing home.

So we began making sourdough with simple, honest ingredients and a slower process. What started in our kitchen is now something we’re proud to share with our community.

Thank you for supporting small, local, and real.

— jad and pia ♡

FAQ

What makes sourdough different?

Our bread is naturally leavened with a living starter and fermented slowly instead of being rushed with commercial yeast.

Where do you bake?

We bake in small batches locally. Pickup and ordering details will be posted here as we finalize them.

How should I store my bread?

Keep it at room temperature for short-term storage. For longer storage, slice and freeze it, then toast or warm slices as needed.

Do you offer focaccia every week?

Focaccia is part of our current menu, but quantities are limited and weekly availability may vary.
